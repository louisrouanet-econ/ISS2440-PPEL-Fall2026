# Optional Online Readings — Week 5

- **Prisoners' Dilemma** — Avinash Dixit and Barry Nalebuff, *Concise Encyclopedia of Economics*. Explains how the prisoners' dilemma captures the tension between individual incentives to defect and the collective gains from cooperation, with applications to pricing, arms races, and other strategic interactions. [https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/PrisonersDilemma.html](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/PrisonersDilemma.html)
